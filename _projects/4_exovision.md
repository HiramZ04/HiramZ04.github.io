An end-to-end ML toolkit for exoplanet detection built on NASA open data, comparing six classifiers (with Optuna hyperparameter tuning) and shipped as a public Streamlit web app — all in under 48 hours. **Regional 1st place and Global Nominee** at the NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2025.

**Stack:** scikit-learn, Optuna, UMAP, Streamlit, Python.
