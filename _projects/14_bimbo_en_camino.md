Delivery drivers for Grupo Bimbo make dozens of stops daily with no real-time decision support — which stores to prioritize, what to restock, how much to leave. **BIMBO en Camino** solves that: an intelligent distribution assistant that rides along on the route and helps drivers make better calls in real time.

Built at the **Swift Changemakers National Hackathon 2026** in Mexico City, organized by **Enactus México** with **Apple** as sponsor. The challenge was proposed by **Grupo Bimbo**, one of the largest baking companies in the world.

The app integrates:
- **Route optimization** — prioritizes stops based on demand signals and delivery history
- **Inventory forecasting** — predicts what each store is likely to need and suggests quantities
- **Voice interface** — hands-free interaction so drivers stay focused on the road
- **Route tracking** — keeps a full log of deliveries, confirmations, and exceptions

The real challenge was connecting ML, LLMs, voice interfaces, route optimization, and logistics operations into a single practical tool — not a demo, but something a driver could actually use on a shift.

**Stack:** Swift, SwiftUI, MapKit, CoreML, LLM integration, on-device inference.

**Team:** Hugo Manzano · Hiram Zúñiga · Luis Bremer
