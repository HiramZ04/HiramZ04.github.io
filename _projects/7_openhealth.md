A health resource platform built at the **San Diego Big Data Hackathon 2025** (SDSU, sponsored by Sharp & HDMA). OpenHealth connects users in crisis to nearby shelters, medical aid, and mental health resources through an interactive map and a guided mental health triage flow.

**Stack:** React, geolocation API, open health datasets, data visualization.
