A conversational study assistant powered by a large language model with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Upload your notes or textbooks and StudyBot summarizes content, generates practice quizzes, and answers questions grounded in your material — with citations to the source document.

**Stack:** LLM, RAG, vector store, Python.
