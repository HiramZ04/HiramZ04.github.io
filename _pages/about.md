I'm a **computer vision** researcher and engineer. My work applies vision models to problems that matter: **medical imaging** — retrieval-augmented vision-language models for leukemia cell classification, CNN benchmarks for Alzheimer's detection in MRI — and **assistive robotics** — human-following robots for the elderly and navigation aids for blind users. I'm increasingly focused on **generative models and diffusion**, and I keep an active side interest in **quantum machine learning**.

I've authored **5 peer-reviewed papers and manuscripts** (3 first-author), including two oral presentations at [SPIE Optics + Photonics 2026](https://spie.org/conferences-and-exhibitions/optics-and-photonics) and a first-author paper at KES 2026. Beyond the lab, I build and ship: event-driven systems on AWS (demoed to AWS engineers at the Amazon San Diego office) and rapid prototypes at hackathons — podium finishes at **MIT iQuHACK**, **Yale YQuantum**, and **NASA Space Apps**.

I'm currently preparing applications for graduate programs in the US and open to ML engineering and research collaborations. Here are my [industry resume](/assets/pdf/hiram_resume_industry.pdf) and [academic CV](/assets/pdf/hiram_cv_academic.pdf).
