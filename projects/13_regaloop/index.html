RegaLoop — iOS Donation App

iOS app using CoreML on-device inference to classify donations from photos and connect donors with shelters. Swift Changemakers Hackathon 2026 — qualified for national phase.

Donating clothes or food should take 30 seconds, not a phone call and a drive across the city. RegaLoop automates the entire flow: take a photo of the item you want to donate, CoreML classifies it on-device and pre-fills the form, you pick a drop-off point or shelter, and you’re done.

Built at the Swift Changemakers Hackathon 2026 at CETYS Universidad Tijuana — a human-centered AI hackathon organized by Enactus México and Community Changemakers. Our team qualified for the national phase in Mexico City.

The technical focus was on-device inference and MLOps for iOS: deploying a trained CoreML model directly inside the app so classification happens locally, with no server round-trip and no privacy tradeoffs.

Stack: Swift, SwiftUI, CoreML, MapKit, on-device ML inference.

Team: Hugo Manzano · Hiram Zúñiga · Luis Bremer
