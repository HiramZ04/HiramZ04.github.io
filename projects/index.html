projects

Selected work outside research — mostly ML engineering and applied AI, several built at hackathons.

QAOA Portfolio Optimizer

Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm applied to premium investment portfolio allocation. YQuantum (Yale) — 3rd place.

Navi — Assistive Navigation for the Blind

Real-time perception-to-voice navigation aid combining YOLO, LiDAR, VLM, and LLM. HackMerced XI (UC Merced).

OpenHealth

Health resource platform connecting users to shelters, medical aid, and mental health support. San Diego Big Data Hackathon (SDSU) 2025.

ExoVision — ML for Exoplanet Detection

ML pipeline comparing 6 classifiers on NASA open data to detect exoplanets. NASA Space Apps Challenge — Regional 1st place, Global Nominee.

AI Book Explorer

NLP pipeline using sentence embeddings, UMAP, and LLM-assisted clustering for thematic analysis. Capstone with Cal State LA.

TB & Pneumonia Classifier

CNN-based web app for tuberculosis and pneumonia detection from chest X-rays, with patient management dashboard.

StudyBot

LLM-powered study assistant with RAG — summarizes documents, generates quizzes, and answers questions from your own material.

Medical Insurance Prediction

ML-powered web app predicting medical insurance charges from demographic and health features.

AWS Message Router

Serverless message routing pipeline using API Gateway, SQS, Lambda, EventBridge, Step Functions, SNS, DynamoDB, and S3. Presented at an AWS meetup hosted at the Amazon San Diego office.

Quantum Rings — MIT iQuHACK 2026

ML models predicting quantum circuit runtime and fidelity threshold from QASM features. MIT iQuHACK 2026 — 3rd place, Quantum Rings Challenge.

RegaLoop — iOS Donation App

iOS app using CoreML on-device inference to classify donations from photos and connect donors with shelters. Swift Changemakers Hackathon 2026 — qualified for national phase.

BIMBO en Camino

iOS distribution assistant for Bimbo delivery drivers — route optimization, inventory forecasting, and voice interface. Swift Changemakers National Hackathon 2026, Mexico City. Enactus México & Apple.
