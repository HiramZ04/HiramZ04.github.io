Hiram Zuniga

Computer Vision Researcher · B.S. Computer Science, CETYS University

Tijuana, Mx

zuniga.hiram@cetys.edu.mx

I’m a computer vision researcher and engineer. My interests are mostly on medical applications, such as medical imaging. I’m increasingly focused on generative models and diffusion where I am researching how Generative Models can enhance medical diagnosis and model training.

During my last year at undergrad, I started working with Ulises Orozco-Rosas as Research Assistant where I have been working actively on 5 peer-reviewed papers (3 first-author) on medical imaging, medical diagnosis and robots using deep learning. Outside research I enjoy working with machine learning, i have developed AWS applications and demoed them at the Amazon San Diego office, and have participated in hackathons with podium finishes at MIT iQuHACK, Yale YQuantum, and NASA Space Apps.

I’m currently preparing applications for graduate programs and open to ML engineering and research collaborations. Here are my industry resume and academic CV.
